# lucaqrcodemaker

A versatile QR code generator built in Python that lets you create **infinitely reusable QR codes** from any link. Perfect for businesses, personal projects, or anywhere you need dynamic, scannable codes.

## Features

✨ **Infinitely Reusable** - Generate QR codes that can be used unlimited times  
🔗 **Any Link** - Works with any URL or web link  
🎨 **Custom Logo** - Optionally embed your logo or image in the center of the QR code  
⚡ **Fast & Simple** - Quick generation with minimal setup  
🐍 **Python-Powered** - Built with Python for reliability and extensibility

## What Makes This Special?

This QR code maker creates standard QR codes that never expire and can be scanned as many times as needed. Unlike some QR code services that limit scans or require subscriptions, the QR codes you generate here are yours to use forever.

Additionally, you can personalize your QR codes by adding a logo in the middle, making them perfect for:
- Business cards
- Marketing materials
- Product packaging
- Event tickets
- Restaurant menus
- And much more!

## Use Cases

- **Businesses**: Create branded QR codes for your website, social media, or promotions
- **Events**: Generate codes for event registration, tickets, or information
- **Personal**: Make QR codes for your portfolio, blog, or contact information
- **Marketing**: Design eye-catching codes with your company logo
